Momentum Stones

Stones slide on frictionless ice. You plan pushes for your green stones, then press Run and see where everything is when the clock stops.

The stones

🟢
Green: yours
Move them and plan a push for each one: how hard (force), which way, when it starts, and how long it lasts.
Worth 0 points
🟡
Gold: get them in
Each gold stone is worth its mass in points, times the zone it ends in.
+ mass × zone
🔴
Red: get them out
Each red stone costs its mass in points, times the zone it ends in.
− mass × zone

Zones

  • Center circle ×3, the ring between the circles ×1, and outside the big circle ×0. A stone counts in the zone its center is in.
  • Example: a 4 kg gold stone in the center is worth +12. A 2 kg red stone in the ring costs −2.

How a round works

  • Setup: drag a green stone to move it. Drag the tip of its arrow to aim and set the force, or use the controls under the ice. Dashed lines show where each stone would end up if nothing hit it.
  • You only have so much impulse (force × time, in N·s) to share between your green stones. A push changes a stone's speed by Δv = F·Δt ÷ m, so the same push moves a light stone more than a heavy one.
  • Press ▶ Run. Every stone starts with its own velocity, your pushes happen on schedule, and stones bounce off each other. There's no friction, so a moving stone keeps moving until something hits it.
  • When the clock reaches the level's time, everything freezes and the stone points are counted.
  • ⚡ Efficiency bonus: impulse you don't use makes your score bigger. Bonus = stone points × the share of the budget left unused. Example: 6 stone points using 2.5 of 10 N·s leaves 75% unused, so the bonus is +4.5 and the score is 10.5. There's no bonus unless your stone points are above 0.
  • Earn ★ to unlock the next level. ★★★ takes the right result and a thrifty plan.
  • The log keeps every run, so you can compare plans.
