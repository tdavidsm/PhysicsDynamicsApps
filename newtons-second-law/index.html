Newton's 2nd Law Lab

Find out how the force on an object and its mass work together to set its acceleration.

The stages

1 · 🛒 Push the Cart
Three pushers and three baby animals in shopping carts. Watch each push and its velocity–time graph, then rate the acceleration Slow, Medium or Fast.
Clears: all 9 cells right.
2 · 🧪 Cart & Pulley
Task A: move masses from the cart to the hanger (same total mass, more force). Task B: add blocks to the cart (same force, more mass).
Clears: 5 trials in each task, then the check questions.
3 · 📈 Find the Law
Step through graphs of your own data and work out the equation that links a, F and m.
Clears: every step answered.
4 · 🏁 Race Day
Two carts, different masses. Predict which reaches the end first, or if it's a tie.
Clears: 5 right before 3 wrong. Then you get your completion code.

How it works

  • Finish a stage to unlock the next one. Your progress is saved on this device.
  • The lab track has no friction, and g = 9.8 m/s². The weight of the hanging mass is the force that pulls the cart.
  • The app measures the slope of each velocity–time graph and puts the acceleration in your data table.
